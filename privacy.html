Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

1. Who we are

Kailash ("we", "us") is an online retreat service for burnout. The controller of your personal data is Nikita, Warsaw, Poland. Contact: contact.wizard.io@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • Your email: you enter it to activate the 7-day gift for our first members, or to sign in. We send a one-time code to it — no password is needed. We keep the email (with the date and where on the site you entered it) even if you never confirm the code, so we can link your path and any purchase to you. If you created an account with a password earlier, it is stored encrypted by our authentication provider; we never see it.
  • Usage data: your plan, which session comes next, how many sessions and minutes you have completed, the days you practised and any promo code you used — so your progress follows you across devices.
  • An account without email: when you start a session or claim the free week, we create an account with a random ID only — no name, no email. It keeps your progress and your free week in this browser. You can add your email later to keep them on any device.
  • Consents: the date you accepted the Terms. We do not send marketing emails.
  • Activity on the site: simple steps such as "opened the site", "started a session", "finished a session", with a random ID created in your browser, the type of device (phone or desktop) and the site you came from. It contains no name and no email; if you enter your email, we can connect these steps to it. It helps us see where the site is unclear.
  • Payment data: handled entirely by our payment provider. We receive confirmation of purchase, never your card details.

Your written reflections and the feelings you choose in "Ask Shiva" stay in your browser on your device. They are not sent to us.

3. Why we use it (legal basis)

  • To provide your account, free sessions and purchased retreats — performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To understand, from activity on the site, where the site is unclear and improve it — our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time by writing to us.
  • To keep records required by tax and accounting law — legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

4. Who processes your data

  • Supabase — account and database hosting.
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting.
  • Resend — sends account emails (sign-in codes, confirmation, password reset).
  • Cloudflare — domain and DNS, forwarding of emails sent to our address, and privacy-friendly visit statistics (see section 7).
  • Google Fonts — delivers the site's fonts; your browser connects to Google's servers.
  • Lemon Squeezy — payments and invoices.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, transfers rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time under Account → Delete account; your account and progress are erased immediately, and any remaining backup copies within 30 days. Records we must keep by law (such as invoices) are kept for the period the law requires.

6. Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of your data, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time. Write to contact.wizard.io@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We use your browser's local storage to remember your progress, your sound preference, the email you entered, the random activity ID and your sign-in session. This is strictly necessary for the service to work.

To see how many people visit and which pages they open, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies, does not use local storage, does not build a profile of you and does not track you across other sites.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you by email.
