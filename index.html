Session 1 · Bhagavad Gita 2.47

Your right is to the work alone — never to its fruits.

Read it slowly. Once more. Then continue.

What it means for you

You did the work.
The rest isn't yours.

The unanswered email, the review, the result — Krishna tells Arjuna to act fully and let go of the outcome. Today that means: you showed up. What happens next can wait until morning.
