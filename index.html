Tonight's evening · 10 minutes

Leave the office
at the door.

Sit somewhere comfortable. Put your phone on silent, but keep the sound of this page on — a temple bell will guide you. Four short steps, then you're free.

Verse · Bhagavad Gita 2.47

Your right is to the work alone — never to its fruits.

Read it slowly. Once more. Then continue.

What it means tonight

You did the work.
The rest isn't yours.

The unanswered email, the review, the result — Krishna tells Arjuna to act fully and let go of the outcome. Tonight, that means: you showed up. What happens next can wait until morning.

Practice · one minute of breath
Press begin

In for 4, out for 6. Six slow breaths.

Chant · 9 beads

Om Namah Shivaya

Say it softly and tap the Om with each repetition.
